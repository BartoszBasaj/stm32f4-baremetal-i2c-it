How to read the waveform

SCL is the clock (grey), SDA the data (black). A bit is valid while SCL = 1; SDA changes only while SCL = 0, except for START (SDA falls while SCL = 1) and STOP (SDA rises while SCL = 1).

A byte is 8 bits, MSB first, plus a 9th clock where the receiver pulls SDA low (ACK) or leaves it high (NACK). Both lines are open-drain with pull-ups.

Orange dashed = clock stretching: the STM32 holds SCL low while ADDR or BTF is set, until the ISR has done its job.
